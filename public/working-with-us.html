Process

Every project starts with a conversation. Whether you are opening a hotel, a restaurant, a store or a wellness space, this is how we take it from first idea to opening day.

How a project comes together

  1. First conversation

    We listen to what the space needs to do, who it is for, and the budget and timeline you are working with.

  2. Concept

    We come back with a direction: how the space flows, how it feels, and the materials and light that will carry it.

  3. Design development

    Together we refine every detail, from joinery and lighting to finishes, furniture and signage.

  4. Drawings and specifications

    We prepare the drawings and specifications that contractors and vendors build from.

  5. On site

    We stay involved through construction, reviewing samples and answering questions so the space is built as designed.

  6. Styling and handover

    We add the final layer of art, objects and soft furnishings, and hand over a space ready to welcome people.

Who we work with

  • Hotels, resorts and homestays
  • Restaurants, cafes and bars
  • Retail stores and brand spaces
  • Spas, studios and wellness centres

Helpful to have ready

None of this is essential for a first chat, but it helps us get to the point quickly:

  • Photos or drawings of the site
  • A rough budget and when you hope to open
  • A few places or images you love, and why
