questions

How early should we bring you in?

Before you sign for a site. Ceiling height, drainage and ventilation decide which treatments and classes a space can hold.

Do you design small studios?

Yes. In a single-floor studio every metre counts, from the shoe rack to the space for talking after class.

Do you work outside Bengaluru?

Yes. We are based in Indiranagar, Bengaluru, and take projects across India.

How are your fees set?

By scope and area, agreed after a site visit and a first brief. We do not publish fixed fees.

Do you build as well as design?

We design, then stay on site through delivery: site visits, contractor checks, styling and handover. Construction is carried out by contractors appointed for the project.
